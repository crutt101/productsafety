Bread & Glyphosate

Every third-party lab test on glyphosate in retail bread we could find, brand by brand, in parts per billion.

Why is a weed killer in bread?

Glyphosate is the active ingredient in Roundup and the most heavily used herbicide in history. It ends up in bread not through GMO wheat (there is none on the market) but through pre-harvest desiccation — spraying wheat and oats shortly before harvest to dry the crop for earlier, more uniform harvesting. Residues on the grain survive milling and baking.

In 2015 the WHO's International Agency for Research on Cancer classified glyphosate as Group 2A — "probably carcinogenic to humans." The US EPA disagrees and considers it unlikely to be carcinogenic at dietary levels; Bayer has nonetheless paid billions to settle Roundup cancer lawsuits. Whole-wheat breads tend to test higher than white breads because residues concentrate in the bran.

How to read the numbers

  • 10 ppb — typical lab detection limit (LC-MS/MS); "ND" means below this.
  • 160 ppb — EWG's child-protective health benchmark. Several popular breads exceed it.
  • 10,000 ppb (10 ppm) — EU maximum residue level for wheat grain.
  • 30,000 ppb (30 ppm) — US EPA tolerance for wheat grain. Every bread ever tested is far below this, so all of these products are legal.

Lowering your glyphosate exposure

Patterns across all three reports:

  • Certified organic usually — but not always — tests lower. Glyphosate is prohibited in organic farming, yet drift and cross-contamination happen: 365 Organic Artisan Sourdough still tested at 23 ppb. Organic breads that tested ND include Rudi's Organic Rocky Mountain Sourdough and Dave's Killer Bread Organic Sprouted Thin-Sliced.
  • Sprouted-grain breads were consistently among the lowest (Angelic Bakehouse 12 ppb, Silver Hills ND).
  • Conventional 100% whole wheat was consistently the worst category — residues ride on the bran that white flour discards.
  • White breads are a mixed bag: Wonder Classic White hit 173 ppb while Sara Lee Artesano and Pepperidge Farm Farmhouse White were ND in the same Florida report.
