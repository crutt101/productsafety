How scoring works

One number. Real criteria.

Every product starts at 100. We deduct points for each ingredient in our hazard database, weighted by the strength of the evidence against it.

Carcinogen check

Ingredients classified by IARC (Groups 1, 2A, 2B) or flagged by the US National Toxicology Program — potassium bromate, Red 3, BHA, aspartame, titanium dioxide and more.

Banned elsewhere

Additives that are legal in the US but banned or restricted in the EU, UK, Japan, Canada and beyond — azodicarbonamide, BVO, bleached flour, propylparaben and friends.

Score out of 100

Severity-weighted deductions produce a single score and letter grade, plus a highlighted ingredient list so you can see exactly what triggered each flag.
